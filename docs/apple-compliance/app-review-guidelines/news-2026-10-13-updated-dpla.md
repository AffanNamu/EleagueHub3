## Updated Apple Developer Program License Agreement now available

October 13, 2026

The [Apple Developer Program License Agreement](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/) has been revised to support new features and updated policies, and to provide clarification. Please review the changes below and [sign in to your account](https://developer.apple.com/account/) to accept the updated terms.

- Section 3.2: Updated security and related requirements for using Apple’s software and services.
- Definitions, Section 3.3.8(j): Specified terms for use of the Live Assistance API.
- Definitions, Section 3.3.8(k): Specified terms for use of the App Replacement Entitlement.
- Definitions, Definitions, Sections 6.4, 6.4.1, 6.4.2: Specified terms for iOS app widgets on Apple HomeView and availability of iOS apps for CarPlay on Apple HomeView.
- Schedule 2, Sections 1.1(b), 1.2(d)(iv), 3.1, 3.8(c): Specified terms for offering multiple seats for a subscription through Group Purchases.
- Schedule 3, Sections 1.1(b), 1.2(d)(iii), 3.1, 3.8(a), 3.8(c): Specified terms for offering subscriptions to Apple Business and Apple School Manager organizations through Volume Purchasing.
- Schedule 2, Section 3.3(a); Schedule 3, Section 3.3(a): Specifies that Apple will report legally required information such as tax, sales, and contact details to tax authorities.
- Schedule 2, Section 3.3(b); Schedule 3, Section 3.3(b): Restricts the use of any customer tax information shared by Apple with developers to only those purposes mandated by law.

Translations of the updated agreement will be available on the [Apple Developer website](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/) within one month.
