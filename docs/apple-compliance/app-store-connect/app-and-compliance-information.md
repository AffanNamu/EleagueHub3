# App and compliance information

*App and compliance information* refers to the properties that are shared across the platforms added to an app. Some of these properties are set when you [add the app to your account](/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/add-a-new-app), and later, you can [edit app information](/help/app-store-connect/create-an-app-record/view-and-edit-app-information) as needed based on the [app's status](/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-and-submission-statuses).
