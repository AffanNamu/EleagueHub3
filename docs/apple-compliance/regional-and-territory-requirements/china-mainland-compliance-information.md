# View China mainland compliance information

## Anti Telecom Fraud Law compliance information

If you're an organization based in China mainland, the [Anti Telecom Fraud Law](https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2022-09/02/content_5708119.htm) and [Cybersecurity Law](https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2016-11/07/content_5129723.htm) require Apple to collect and verify identification information for the purpose of displaying on your apps’ product pages. Apple will automatically display the following additional information your app's product page on the App Store in China mainland:

### Organizations:

- **Company name in Chinese.** The name associated with your Dun & Bradstreet (D-U-N-S) profile is automatically displayed.

- **China Unified Social Credit Identifier (USCI).** The USCI associated with your D-U-N-S Number will automatically display. If you need to update the USCI associated with your D-U-N-S Number, or if you don't have a D-U-N-S number associated with your developer account but wish to display your USCI, [contact D&B](/support/D-U-N-S/) to update or create your D-U-N-S account information. (If you have a D-U-N-S number with the correct USCI, and don’t see the USCI reflected on your apps product page, [contact us](/contact).)

Learn more about the [Anti Telecom Fraud Law](https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2022-09/02/content_5708119.htm) and [Cybersecurity Law](https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2016-11/07/content_5129723.htm).

### View Anti Telecom Fraud Law compliance information

**Required role:** Account Holder or Admin. [View role permissions.](/help/app-store-connect/reference/account-management/role-permissions)

1.  Select Business at the top of the page.

2.  On the Agreements tab, scroll down to the Compliance section.

3.  Click on China Mainland.

4.  Confirm your information, and click Done.

## AI-generated content and Security Assessment compliance

If your app is available on the App Store in China mainland, local laws and regulations require you to declare whether your app offers regulated features within China mainland and provide approved compliance documentation.

You must submit declarations and supporting documentation if your app offers any of the following in China mainland:

- **AI-generation or Synthetic services:** In accordance with the Measures for Labeling AI-Generated or Synthetic Content law, if your app offers services such as AI chatbots, image or video generation, or voice and face alteration, you must declare whether your app offers these services and provide approved Large Language Model (LLM) or algorithm(Algo) filing information. If your app’s AI features are powered by someone else's technology, you must also provide an authorization or usage agreement.

  [Learn more about the Measures for Labeling AI-Generated or Synthetic Content Law](http://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm)

- **Features requiring a Security Assessment:** In accordance with applicable local laws and regulations, if your app includes features such as distributing AI-generated or Synthetic content or enabling end-to-end encrypted instant messaging (EIM), you must submit proof of a completed Security Assessment and a copy of the Security Assessment report from the National Internet Security Management Service Platform.

  [Learn more about the National Internet Security Management Service Platform](https://beian.mps.gov.cn/)

  [Learn more about the Interim Measures for Generative Artificial Intelligence Service Management](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm)

  [Learn more about the regulations on the Security Assessment of Internet Information Services with Public Opinion Attributes or Social Mobilization Ability](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2018-11/15/c_1123716072.htm)

**Note:**

- All apps available on the App Store in China mainland must complete these compliance declarations:

  - Select **Yes**: If your app offers these features to users in China mainland.

  - Select **No**: If your app does not offer these features, or if they are disabled or geoblocked in China mainland.

- When providing this documentation, please redact any confidential and/or proprietary information.

- Your app will be released in all other selected countries and regions, but it won’t be available on the App Store in China mainland until your compliance information is verified.

- By January 31, 2027, all existing apps in China mainland must have completed and verified compliance declarations to remain available on the App Store.

If you have questions on how to comply with these requirements, please consult the regulatory guidance of the Cyberspace Administration of China or the [National Internet Security Management Service Platform](https://beian.mps.gov.cn/).

### Provide an AI-Generated or Synthetic Content declaration

**Required role:** Account Holder or Admin. [View role permissions.](/help/app-store-connect/reference/role-permissions)

1.  In Apps, select your app from the list.

2.  In the sidebar, under General, click App Compliance.

3.  Next to AI-Generated or Synthetic Content, select Edit.

4.  Follow the prompts to declare whether your app offers AI-generation or Synthetic services in China mainland.

5.  If your app offers AI-generation or Synthetic services provide the following:

    - **Filing details:** The approved filing number and registered filing name for either your Large Language Model (LLM) or your Algorithm (Algo).

    - **Third-party technology authorization (if applicable):** If your app’s AI features are powered by someone else's technology, upload an authorization or usage agreement issued by the technology owner. Because formats vary by provider, ensure your document clearly includes:

      - The registered name and filing number of the foundation model or algorithm (Algo).

      - The provider’s name, explicitly identifying you or your organization as the authorized party.

      - The authorizing entity’s name, which must match the owner of the foundation model or algorithm as listed in the official Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC) approval registry.

6.  Select Save.

    The compliance status updates to Pending Verification. If your app version is approved but your compliance declarations fail verification, your app will be released in all other selected countries and regions, but will remain unavailable on the App Store in China mainland. To resolve the issue, update and save your information on the App Compliance page. Your updated compliance declarations will be reviewed when you submit your next app version to App Review. Once verified and approved, your app will become available on the App Store in China mainland.

### Provide a Security Assessment declaration

**Required role:** Account Holder or Admin. [View role permissions.](/help/app-store-connect/reference/role-permissions)

1.  In Apps, select your app from the list.

2.  In the sidebar, under General, click App Compliance.

3.  Next to Security Assessment, select Edit.

4.  Follow the prompts to declare whether your app includes features in China mainland that require a Security Assessment, such as distributing AI-generated or synthetic content or enabling end-to-end encrypted instant messaging.

5.  If your app includes the features that require a Security Assessment, provide:

    - **Proof of completed Security Assessment:** A screenshot from the National Internet Security Management Service Platform confirming that your assessment has passed review. The screenshot must display your app name, provider name, and a status showing “pass” or “passed review.”

    - **Security Assessment report:** A copy of the completed Security Assessment report. The report must include the app name, provider name, and the official company seal or chop.

6.  Select Save.

    The compliance status updates to Pending Verification. If your app version is approved but your compliance declarations fail verification, your app will be released in all other selected countries and regions, but will remain unavailable on the App Store in China mainland. To resolve the issue, update and save your information on the App Compliance page. Your updated compliance declarations will be reviewed when you submit your next app version to App Review. Once verified and approved, your app will become available on the App Store in China mainland.
