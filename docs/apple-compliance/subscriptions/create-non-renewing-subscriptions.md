# Create non-renewing subscriptions

A non-renewing subscription gives customers access to a service or content for a specific period of time (for example, access to a magazine for one month). To offer non-renewing subscriptions, enter product details in App Store Connect.

**Note:** It may take up to 1 hour for changes you make to product metadata to appear in the sandbox environment.

[Learn how to create a non-renewing subscription using the App Store Connect API.](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreconnectapi/managing-in-app-purchases)

1.  In Apps, select the app you want to view.

2.  In the sidebar, under Monetization, click Subscriptions.

3.  Scroll down to Non-Renewing Subscriptions, and click Manage.

4.  Click the add button (+).

5.  Enter a Reference Name and Product ID, then click Create.

6.  [Set a price](/help/app-store-connect/manage-in-app-purchases/set-a-price-for-an-in-app-purchase#set-a-price-for-an-in-app-purchase) and [add localizations](/help/app-store-connect/manage-in-app-purchases/view-and-edit-in-app-purchase-information#add-and-remove-localizations).

7.  Click Save.

**Note:** Your first non-renewing subscription must be submitted with a new app version. After creating your subscription, select Add for Review and include an app version in your submission to have it reviewed together with your app. Once your first non-renewing subscription has been approved, you can submit additional non-renewing subscriptions without a new app version. [Learn more about submitting subscriptions for review.](/help/app-store-connect/manage-submissions-to-app-review/overview-of-submitting-for-review)
