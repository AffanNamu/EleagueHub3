## Now available: Retention messages for subscriptions

November 4, 2026

![Two iPhones showing a subscription cancellation screen with a developer message, one with an image and one with a discount offer.](https://devimages-cdn.apple.com/wwdc-services/articles/images/1A6769C9-CED6-4B3D-B1C8-2FD454186C72/2048.jpeg)

Reduce cancellations with retention messages that remind subscribers of your subscription's value as they decide whether to cancel. Retention messages appear in the cancellation flow and can include text, images, or a special offer.

In App Store Connect, you can configure retention messages for your auto-renewable subscriptions, including subscriber eligibility and the wait time between offers. With the [Retention Messaging API](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging/), you can choose which message to show each subscriber based on their subscription details. API access is available by request.

[Configure retention messages in App Store Connect](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-subscriptions/configure-retention-messages/)

[Learn about auto-renewable subscriptions](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/subscriptions/)
